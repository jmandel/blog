I ran this past an AI reviewer and it asked: "which spec are you pointing to?" Of course my implicit but intentional answer is "none, yet." I guess I should explain.

Vendors, left to themselves, have little reason to build something that unbundles a function they currently control. That's not an indictment; it's just how market positions work. No one rushes to commoditize their own gate. And so the demand signal that would normally pull a standard into existence never forms.A functional certification requirement changes that equation. It creates the incentive that the market on its own won't. Customers get a clear thing to expect, vendors get a clear reason to build it (and build it together, by collaborating on an open spec).

Am I thinking about SMART Permission Tickets? Sure, I hope that will be one possibility. But picking a technology today isn't the point. The goal here is to define the problem space and get the community exploring solutions together.
