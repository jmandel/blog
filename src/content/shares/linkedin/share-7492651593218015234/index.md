Excited to be co-leading this work on improving consumer access to imaging data. I will editorialize from my perspective: imaging access is a problem we need to solve *within health systems* before we make real progress at the network level. That is: why can't I access images via SMART on {{FHIR + DICOM Web}} APIs from my provider today? We've made good strides on API and authz design in Health Level Seven International Argonaut and you can see an annotated demo at https://lnkd.in/gChzvKgf

> **Morris Panner** ([original on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7492546580294610944/)):
>
> We are off and running!
>
> A little over 10 days ago, we unveiled the national commitment to #Ditchthedisk at U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) in Washington, DC. Under the leadership of Deputy Administrator and Chief Product Officer Amy Gleason and Chief Patient Experience and Innovation Officer Anthony Polizzi, eliminating CDs and transforming to digital is moving to be national policy.
>
> Honored to be asked to co-lead the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services #Ditchthedisk Working Group with Didi Davis of The Sequoia Project and Josh Mandel, MD of Aledade and partnering with Ryan Howells of Leavitt Partners and Morgan Taylor of CMS.
>
> We kicked off our first meeting and look forward to establishing the group and giving life to the mission statement which is:
>
> XII. Diagnostic Imaging Acceleration ("Ditch the Disk") Workgroup
> Objective:
> Make imaging data retrievable across organizations, within clinical workflows, and by patients completely, quickly, and without friction, eliminating physical media where digital exchange is possible.
>
